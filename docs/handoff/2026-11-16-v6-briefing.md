# Briefing for schematic / PCB / constraints sessions — SPEC V6.0 (2026-11-16)

The user approved a redesign on 2026-11-16. Read `SPEC.md` (V6.1) and decisions **0009** (rear controller board) and **0010** (24 V) first, then CLAUDE.md for the house rules. This note says what to do, not why.

## What changed
1. **All edge boards are passive** (2-layer, one design per shape, R1 codes 0–5 as BOM variants).
   - The MCU, CAN, regulators, 5 V converter, LED switch and level shifter move to a **small 4-layer controller board**.
   - The controller board lies flat at the rear of the light chamber, on the printed back plate.
   - Its parts face the wall, in a back-plate pocket. The chamber side is white solder mask.
   - It is **drag-soldered at 90°** to ≈10 pads on the **code-0 edge board's** inward face. Nets: +24V, GND, +5V to the LEDs, LED data out, LED return in, CANH, CANL, SENSE, DRIVE, spare.
   - **Shape (SPEC V6.1):** one design must fit every panel, so it is sized for the triangle. It is a trapezoid with 60° sides, base ≤ ≈72 mm along the code-0 board, depth ≈20–25 mm (≈1,200–1,400 mm²). A full-edge-length rectangle would not fit a triangle.
2. **Edge board height target 10–15 mm** (estimate 12–13 mm). This supersedes H = 22 mm (0005), the active/passive split (0006) and the active-board-only in-pad via rule (0007).
3. **Minimum constraints.** The user explicitly wants no artificial rules: lanes and the bus may go on either face at any height. Keep only rules with a stated reason:
   - **Contact pads on the exterior face, inside the springs' zone:** the seam bridge presses there.
   - **GND pads closest to the wall edge:** mating order.
   - **DRIVE never next to +24V:** a short there is destructive.
   - **+24V never beside +5V without GND or a gap between them:** a short would put 24 V on every LED.
   - **LED positions shared by all shapes:** set by the optics.
   - **1.6 mm thickness:** sets the seam inset and corner geometry.

   If you add a constraint, write its reason next to it. If one has no reason, drop it, and you may question the user's own rules too.
4. **24 V bus** (0010). On the controller board:
   - **TPS54360B** buck (C524806), 24 → 5 V, **always on**.
   - **P-MOSFET LED-rail switch** gated from PA0 (LED_EN), default off. Part to be chosen.
   - **TCAN1044VDRQ1** (C1852061, ±58 V bus fault); VCC from +5V, VIO from 3V3.
   - **SMAJ28A** on +24V.
   - **HT7533 fed from +5V.** The 68 Ω series resistor is removed.
   - **Re-size and re-rate R10/R11** for 24 V faults, and pick a CAN TVS with a standoff above 24 V.
   - Everything on +24V must be rated above the ≈45 V clamp.
   - +12V becomes +24V on the contacts, corners and bridges.
5. **Soldering preference:** small parts are pasted and reflowed. Hand drag-soldering of the corner joints and the controller joint is fine.
